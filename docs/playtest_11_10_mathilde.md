Mathilde, 10:57am to 11:30am, Basel, Switzerland

Shared 11 × 11 grid, arrow, touch and click movement, hidden target for each player. Each target is visible to everyone except its owner, and players guide each other.


Arriving with nothing to do is boring. “What’s the point of meeting on an 11 × 11 grid? If I join and have to wait my turn doing nothing, I get bored.” Something fun needs to happen, or there needs to be something to do, from the moment you join.

Targets should mean something. An ordinary square isn’t motivating. She’d rather find memories or something personal: “play to discover memories and feel connected.”

The conversation is the interesting part. “It’s more interesting to talk about us.” She suggested small dares or prompts, such as recording a voice note or taking a photo, with a recap at the end, like a reel or a board.

The squares in between could hold content. Players could leave a drawing or a message on them for others to find.

Movement is easy to miss. On a call you don’t watch the screen all the time, so she wanted a trace of each player’s path, or at least their previous position.

Getting everyone together at the same time is hard. She suggested teams or an asynchronous option.
